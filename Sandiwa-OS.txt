Sandiwa OS — HOA Edition
Product brief

Sandiwa, from “Isang Diwa” (one spirit): shared accountability across homeowners, board, and property staff.

This repository is HOA-only. Terminology, seed data, and demos target subdivisions and condominiums (Greenfield Village HOA, Sunrise Condominium HOA). Cooperatives and generic associations are out of scope. This is not a government resident registry or LGU compliance system.

Who it serves

- Buyer: HOA board, property manager, or a developer offering a community portal.
- Platform admin — register HOAs, plans, and feature toggles on the main domain (`/platform`).
- Org staff — Operations Console (`/console`): org_admin, manager, committee_lead, staff.
- Homeowner / unit occupant — Impact landing (`/`) and member portal (`/portal/*`).

Three surfaces

- Impact — member-facing org site: landing, announcements, EN/TL toggle, Listen on announcements / alerts / statement, member home for requests, statement, payments, votes, clearances, and alerts (`/alerts`).
- Operations Console — authenticated staff desk. Modules appear only when the org’s plan and feature flags allow them, and only for roles in the permission matrix.
- Platform — main domain only: HOA directory, org create/edit, feature toggles, light analytics. Demo data reset is local-only.

Plugins (per org)

Feature flags on each HOA, also capped by plan (basic / standard / premium):

- intake — member requests; staff triage and assignment
- payments — dues, manual payment submit (GCash / Maya / bank / cash / check), clearances, aging
- ledger — accounts, disbursement vouchers, entries
- polls — one vote per unit
- broadcasts — portal alerts (info / warning / emergency)
- announcements, staff_management, landing_editor — console tools

Seed after `pnpm db:setup`:

- greenfield-hoa — premium; ledger off; other plugins on
- sunrise-condo — standard; ledger on; broadcasts off

Roles

- org_admin — full console, including staff management
- manager — ops and money modules (not staff_management)
- committee_lead — intake (assigned cases) and polls; can create polls, cannot close them
- staff — intake, payments, announcements; can verify payments and review clearances
- member — portal only; can submit a payment on their unit

Not built (reserved env keys only): intake auto-triage, PayMongo / Xendit capture, SMS delivery.

Sandiwa OS is the operating desk for Filipino homeowners associations: units, dues, requests, votes, and emergencies in one place — usable in Tagalog with large tap targets, and strict enough for a board that needs an audit trail.
